Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

Memo Flip ("the App") is a memory puzzle game developed by SCHOOL BOY DEVELOPERS ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how Memo Flip handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Memo Flip does not collect, transmit, or share personal information.

The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or location.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Memo Flip may store basic game information locally on your device, such as game progress, settings, scores, or high scores.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

3. Internet Connection

Memo Flip is designed to provide its core gameplay functionality offline.

An internet connection is not required to play the game.

4. Third-Party Services

Memo Flip does not use third-party services to collect or share personal information.

The App does not use advertising services, analytics services, social media services, or other third-party services that collect personal information.

5. Children's Privacy

Memo Flip does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Because the App does not require users to provide personal information, users can play the game without creating an account or submitting personal data.

6. Data Security

Since Memo Flip does not collect or transmit personal information, we do not maintain personal user information on our servers.

Any basic game information stored locally on the device remains under the control of the device and its operating system.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Memo Flip does not maintain personal user data on external servers.

Locally stored game information can generally be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App from the device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will include a revised effective date.
